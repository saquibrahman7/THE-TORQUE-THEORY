Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how The Torque Theory handles information when you visit our automotive website.

Some tools may process information you enter, such as vehicle details, mileage or fuel costs, in your browser. Whether information is saved locally depends on the individual tool's implementation. Do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive information into these tools.

Hosting and technical information

Our hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs for delivery, security and operational purposes. Consult the hosting provider's privacy documentation for details.

External links

Our website may link to third-party websites, including online retailers and social networks. Those websites operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control their data practices.

Affiliate links

If affiliate links are introduced, participating retailers or affiliate programmes may use tracking mechanisms to attribute qualifying visits or purchases. Relevant links and pages will disclose affiliate relationships.

Analytics and cookies

This policy must be updated if we introduce analytics, advertising cookies, embedded content or other tracking technologies. Do not assume those services are active unless they are explicitly added to the website.

Policy updates

We may revise this policy as website features change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact
