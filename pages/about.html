The Torque Theory is an automotive project built to help drivers make more informed decisions about owning, maintaining and upgrading their vehicles.

What we're building

  • Vehicle maintenance and service tracking
  • Fuel economy and trip cost calculators
  • Car comparison and used-car research tools
  • Practical automotive guides and accessory recommendations

Our approach

We aim to make automotive information practical, accessible and useful. Product recommendations should explain what to consider, who a product may suit and what buyers should verify before purchase.
